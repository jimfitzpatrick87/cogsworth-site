Cogsworth

Cogsworth is a small, private household planner that runs on one person's own computer. It reads that person's Gmail and Google Calendar read-only to build a short daily priority list, point out bills, and help with a weekly review.

  • It only reads. It never sends, deletes, labels, archives or edits email or calendar events.
  • Email and calendar content stays on the user's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server run by the developer, and nothing is sold or shared.
  • It is a personal-use app for a single household, not a public service.
